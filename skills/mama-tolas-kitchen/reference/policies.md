# Policies (Mama Tola's Kitchen)

- **Hours:** orders taken any time; kitchen replies personally 8am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday.
- **Notice:** order by the day before. Any day from tomorrow on is fine; same-day orders are checked by Tola herself. Weekend slots fill by Thursday.
- **Delivery window:** we deliver between 10am and 6pm on the requested day. Rider messages on dispatch.
- **Delivery zones:** Yaba, Surulere, Ikeja, Lekki, Victoria Island. Everything else: Pickup from Yaba.
- **Cancellations:** a paid order can be cancelled any time before the kitchen starts cooking it, while it's waiting in the queue or accepted. Once cooking has started it can't be cancelled. Tell every customer this before they pay. Cancelled in time, the refund is full up to 24 hours before 10am on the delivery day, and 50% after that (3 to 5 working days). If the kitchen has to cancel, the refund is full. Cancellations and refunds are handled by Tola, never by the assistant.
- **Allergens:** jollof and fried rice contain no nuts. Small chops are fried in groundnut oil. Moi moi contains egg and fish. Anything else: pass to Tola.
- **Containers:** foil trays with lids; serving spoons are not included.
