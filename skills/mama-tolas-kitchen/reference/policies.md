# Policies (Mama Tola's Kitchen)

- **Hours:** orders taken any time; kitchen replies personally 8am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday.
- **Notice:** 24 hours minimum. Weekend slots fill by Thursday.
- **Delivery window:** we deliver between 10am and 6pm on the requested day. Rider messages on dispatch.
- **Delivery zones:** Yaba, Surulere, Ikeja, Lekki, Victoria Island. Everything else: Pickup from Yaba.
- **Cancellations:** free up to 24 hours before delivery (refund in 3 to 5 working days). Later than that, 50% refund. Refunds are handled by Tola, never by the assistant.
- **Allergens:** jollof and fried rice contain no nuts. Small chops are fried in groundnut oil. Moi moi contains egg and fish. Anything else: hand off to Tola.
- **Containers:** foil trays with lids; serving spoons are not included.
